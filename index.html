General ATS & Best Practice Tips

  • ATS-friendly: Use clean typography, standard section titles, and semantic text hierarchy so parsers read content effortlessly.
  • Tailor to the role: Incorporate keywords from the target job posting directly into your skills and project bullets.
  • Use action verbs & numbers: Start bullets with verbs (Built, Led, Optimized) and quantify results (e.g., 40% speedup, 10k+ users).
  • Keep it concise: 1 page for early-career; up to 2 pages for senior roles or countries expecting 2 pages (UK, Australia).
  • Export options: Use Save Vector PDF (ATS) for machine-readable text or Save Visual PDF for visual presentation with photo.
